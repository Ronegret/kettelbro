# Surface brief: app shell + session player

## Scope
Mode: Operate. Whole mobile-first PWA: login, onboarding, Hoy (dashboard), session player (companion), Progreso, Metas, Perfil.

## Audience, job, constraints
Spanish-speaking people training with 1-3 kettlebells at home, phone propped at a distance, hands sweaty. Job: open, see today's session, train it with live guidance, log it, see progress. Must work offline; Google sign-in only.

## Chosen direction
Loteria de Hierro. Memorable moment: today's session is a collectible loteria card that slams into register (ink layers settle) and the month is a 4x7 tabla marked with beans; a completed week shouts LOTERIA.

## Unresolved
Final font pairing is verified in the browser. Share-card art style follows the card.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Each training day is a collectible loteria card and the month is a tabla marked with beans, so a daily routine feels like a game you want to complete, not a log you must fill. Refuses the dark fitness-app default of progress rings, neon lime accent and rounded cards.
OWN-WORLD: Night-indigo ground (#12122B family) with flat chromolithograph inks used as whole fields: vermilion (power), cobalt (strength), jade (endurance), marigold (hybrid), rose (skill), aqua (recovery). The Hoy screen and the session player are drenched in the day's ink. Cards carry a paper border, corner numerals and a wood-type slab name (Alfa Slab One) over linocut-style two-ink figures; data in a tabular grotesque (Anybody variable, weight rises with effort), body in Bricolage Grotesque. Beans (cocoa ellipses with a highlight) are the only organic shapes. Recognisable with text removed: thick-outline figure on a flat ink field inside a paper border.
STORY: The user understands this is a coach with a plan (day, focus, why), believes it is built on documented protocols (evidence chips per block), and acts: press EMPEZAR, follow one monumental instruction at a time, log, mark the bean, share the card.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone 390x844. Top row: "HOY" + streak count. Centre: the day's card (about 62% of the width of the viewport height band) with number top-left, focus top-right, figure in the middle, slab name along the bottom; below it the line "10x10 EMOM · 16 kg · 28 min" and a full-width primary EMPEZAR button in the thumb zone; below the fold the 4x7 tabla. Standard bottom tab bar with four tabs.
FORM: Assigned direction (index 6 of my grounded list: lotería card tradition), seed key b5d65a04. Raises: weight-of-numerals follows effort (variable-font-specimen), one monumental message per player step (airport signage), timeline blocks whose length equals duration (labanotation).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
